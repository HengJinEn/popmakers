ONE LITTLE BUILD. A BIG SCIENCE DISCOVERY.

Build a catapult.
Discover energy.

Malaysia KSSR Science Year 4 · Unit 7: Energy / Tenaga

Where does the movement come from?

Pull the arm back gently and the rubber band stretches. It stores energy, ready to get the arm and a soft projectile moving when you let go.

  1. 01 · PULL BACK

    Elastic potential energy

    Tenaga keupayaan elastik

    The stretched rubber band stores energy.

  2. 02 · RELEASE

    Kinetic energy

    Tenaga kinetik

    Stored energy becomes movement in the arm and projectile.

  3. 03 · OBSERVE

    Energy changes form

    Perubahan bentuk tenaga

    Energy is conserved. Some also transfers to the surroundings as sound and heat.

Build it. Notice it. Explain it.

  • Recognize stored energy and movement as different forms of energy.
  • Explain how potential energy changes into kinetic energy.
  • Draw, write, or talk about what you observe.

Supports learning in KSSR Year 4 standards 7.1.3–7.1.6. View the curriculum ↗

TRY A LITTLE SCIENCE

A little pull. A little more.
What changes?

Predict which of two gentle pull-back distances will send the soft projectile farther. Keep the same projectile, launch angle, rubber band, and base position. Try each distance three times, measure from the same starting point, and explain what you notice.

Make it a grown-up activity: use a soft pom-pom or foam ball, keep the launch area clear, and aim away from people and animals.

What ages is POP MAKERS for?+

The projects on this page are aimed at ages 6+. A grown-up can help with new builds and small pieces. Confirm the final age guidance on the kit packaging.

What’s in the kit?+

Our planned kit includes 3D-printed connectors, wooden sticks, bottle caps, and a simple instruction booklet. Final quantities and contents will be shared with kit details.

Do I need extra tools?+

The core idea is hands-on snap-together building with connectors and everyday materials. Project ideas that need extra materials will say so in their instructions.

Is it safe for little makers?+

These builds involve small pieces. Use grown-up supervision and follow the final kit’s age and safety instructions. Materials, testing, and safety details will be confirmed before launch.

Can we take builds apart and start again?+

That’s the idea! Snap-together building lets curious makers rethink a shape, try a new connection, and dream up their next creation.

How do I get a kit?+

Tap “Get the Kit” to open our interest form. Let us know you’re interested, and watch for the next kit details.
